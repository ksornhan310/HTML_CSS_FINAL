Flavor Profile

This blend offers a balanced combination of caramel sweetness, roasted nuts, and subtle cocoa notes.

It starts with a smooth aroma and mild sweetness, followed by a richer roasted flavor. The finish is clean with a pleasant lingering coffee taste.

Brewing Recommendation

For the best result, we recommend using:

  1. Coffee: 20 g
  2. Water: 300 ml
  3. Water temperature: 90–95°C
  4. Brewing time: 3–4 minutes

Adjust the coffee-to-water ratio according to your preferred strength.
